    Keom's Seat

    A rowing seat carved from rough wood by Keom, coach of the national team.

    He made it for the hero after the original seat was swallowed by a whale, in the middle of a battle in the Arabian Gulf.

    Four wheels, one gold mark. It still rolls true, and the mark still glows.

    Rests by the water, below the lookout.
